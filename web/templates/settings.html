telltale checks packages from third-party APT repositories (Docker, a PPA, your own) only with a key bound to that repository: the one in the snapshot, a built-in vendor key, or one you add here. A key you add vouches only for the address you give with it and the addresses under it, never for any other repository, and never for the official Debian and Ubuntu archives.
